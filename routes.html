Mostar on foot · Three ways through the old city

Suggested routes

Choose a compact walk by pace and light, then let the Neretva and Stari Most keep you oriented.

01 · Morning 02 · Heritage 03 · Golden hour
01

Easy orientation · Morning

Old Bridge morning loop

Duration
1.5 hours
Stops
4 stops
Best for
First-time visitors
  1. Stari MostStart before the bridge gets busy and take in the full stone arch.
  2. KujundžilukFollow the old bazaar lane past copper workshops and small storefronts.
  3. Koski Mehmed Pasha MosqueClimb for the classic elevated view back toward the bridge and river.
  4. Riverside coffee stopFinish slowly by the Neretva before the midday crowds arrive.
02

Context first · Half day

Ottoman heritage walk

Duration
2.5 hours
Stops
5 stops
Best for
History and craft
  1. Kajtaz HouseBegin with a preserved residential house and its quieter domestic scale.
  2. KujundžilukTrace the craft street toward the river through Mostar's commercial core.
  3. Stari MostCross the bridge as the central hinge between both banks of the old town.
  4. Koski Mehmed Pasha MosquePause in the courtyard and look back across the layered cityscape.
  5. War Photo ExhibitionEnd with recent-history context that changes how the historic quarter is read.
03

Light-led · Late afternoon

Golden hour crossing

Duration
1 hour
Stops
3 stops
Best for
Late light and photos
  1. Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque viewpointArrive while the sun is still high enough to light the western stone facades.
  2. Stari Most crossingCross as the bridge shifts from bright limestone to warmer evening tones.
  3. West-bank riversideFinish below the old town for the bridge silhouette and the last reflected light.

Mostar is compact. These routes are intentionally short enough to leave room for unplanned courtyards, coffee, shade, and a second crossing of the bridge.
